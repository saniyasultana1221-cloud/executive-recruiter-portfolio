# Executive Recruiter Portfolio - Mohammed Muzammil

Executive talent portfolio crafted for my client, a Sr. US IT Bench Sales Recruiter. Built with modern dark glassmorphism, mobile-first UX, and an interactive hotlist lead engine. Proudly made using Antigravity and deployed live on Vercel: https://mohammed-muzammil.vercel.app

## Overview
A high-converting, modern executive portfolio tailored specifically for a Senior US IT Bench Sales Recruiter & Business Development Executive. The platform bridges elite technical consultants with US prime vendors and enterprise clients across C2C, W2, and 1099 engagement models.

---

## Key Highlights
- Executive Glassmorphism Design: Obsidian dark theme with subtle illumination and accessible contrast.
- Mobile-First Architecture: Touch-optimized navigation dock, symmetric viewports, and fast action triggers.
- Consultant Hotlist Lead Generator: Rapid modal enabling US prime vendors to request available on-bench talent directly.
- Technical Domains Showcase: Interactive filtering covering .NET, Java, SQL, Cognos BI, and QA Automation profiles.
- US Tax and Compliance Guide: Visual breakdowns of C2C, W2, 1099, and C2H contract frameworks.
- Production Metadata and SEO: Complete Open Graph social share cards and Schema.org structured data.
